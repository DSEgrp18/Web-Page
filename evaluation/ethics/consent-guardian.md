# Consent: guardian of a participant under 18 (draft)

Read each statement aloud, or give it in the guardian's preferred accessible form. Record each
answer, **yes** or **no**.

1. I have received the information sheet, and my questions have been answered.
2. I understand that my child's taking part is voluntary, and that they, or I, can stop it at
   any time without giving a reason.
3. I understand that my child will also be asked themselves, and that **if they say no, they
   will not take part, whatever I have agreed.**
4. I understand that my child's name will not be stored with what is recorded, and that only
   totals will be published.
5. I agree to my child taking part in this study.
6. *(Optional)* I agree to my child's session being audio-recorded. The recording will be deleted
   after notes are written.

# Consent: participants aged 18 or over (draft)

Read each statement aloud. The participant answers **yes** or **no** to each one. Record the
answers. Every "yes" is needed to take part, except the optional ones.

1. I have had the information sheet read or given to me in a form I can use, and my questions
   have been answered.
2. I understand that taking part is my choice, and that I can stop at any time without giving a
   reason.
3. I understand that my name will not be stored with what is recorded, and that only totals will
   be published.
4. I agree to take part in this study.
5. *(Optional)* I agree to the session being audio-recorded. The recording will be deleted after
   notes are written.
6. *(Optional)* I agree to be contacted about a later session.

# Ethics templates

**Status:** drafts. They need the approving institution's review and wording, legal review
for minors' data (Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022), and a Sinhala
translation checked by a native speaker. **No study involving a person starts before approval.**

| File | For | Given as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `information-sheet.md` | Everyone taking part, and guardians | Read aloud and as an accessible document, before any consent |
| `consent-adult.md` | Participants aged 18 or over, including teachers | Recorded verbal consent, or signed, as the participant prefers |
| `consent-guardian.md` | The guardian of a participant under 18 | As above |
| `assent-student.md` | A participant under 18, in addition to the guardian's consent | Spoken, in plain words |

## Accessibility of the process itself

- Every document is offered in accessible digital form, read aloud, or in braille if requested.
  A blind participant is never asked to sign a paper they cannot read.
- Consent may be recorded verbally, on audio, with a witness, where the institution allows it.
- Withdrawal is as easy as consent: one sentence to the facilitator, at any time, with no reason
  needed.

## Where completed forms go

Nowhere in this repository. Store them as the approving institution directs. The data here is
linked only by a participant code, and the list that connects codes to names is kept separately
and destroyed on the schedule the approval sets.

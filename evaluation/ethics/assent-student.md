# Assent: participant under 18 (draft)

This is said to the student in plain words, **after** the guardian's consent and **before**
starting. Adjust the wording to the student's age. Record their answer.

> We have made a website that reads schoolbooks out loud. We want to see if it is easy to use,
> and where it is hard. We are testing the website, not you, and it does not change your marks.
>
> You will try a few things, like finding a chapter and listening, and tell us what you think as
> you go. It takes about an hour, and you can have a break whenever you like.
>
> You don't have to do this. Your parent said yes, but **it is your choice too**. You can stop
> at any time, just by telling me, and nobody will be upset.
>
> Do you have any questions? Would you like to try it?
